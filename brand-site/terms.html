About AAYNA

AAYNA is an online fashion accessories brand for women in Bangladesh, offering earrings, necklaces, bracelets and rings. Our online shop is preparing to launch. Until it opens, this website provides information about AAYNA only, and no orders can be placed through it.

When the shop opens, customers will be able to browse the collection, create an account, place orders for delivery within Bangladesh and follow their orders.

  • You can request deletion of your account at any time from your account settings or by emailing us.
  • We may suspend or close an account that is used for fraud or misuse of the website.

Orders

  • Placing an order is an offer to buy. Your order is accepted once our team confirms it. We may contact you by phone or email to confirm the details.
  • All products are subject to availability. If an item becomes unavailable after you order, we will contact you.
  • If you need to cancel or change an order, contact us as soon as possible and we will do what we can before it is dispatched.

Prices and payment

  • Any delivery charge is shown at checkout before you place your order.
  • Payment is by Cash on Delivery: you pay the courier when your order arrives.

We deliver within Bangladesh through our courier partner. Please give a complete address and an active phone number, and be available to receive your order. We are not responsible for delays caused by incorrect details or by no one being available at delivery.
