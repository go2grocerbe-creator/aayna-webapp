Launch target:

A simple Bangladesh-focused women's accessories ecommerce launch.



Check:

\- Home page

\- Category pages

\- Product detail pages

\- Cart/order form

\- Contact page

\- Delivery information

\- Return/exchange policy

\- Privacy policy

\- BDT pricing

\- Mobile speed

\- Product images

\- SEO title/meta descriptions

\- Open Graph tags

\- canonical URLs

\- sitemap.xml

\- robots.txt

\- Product structured data

\- Admin/internal noindex

\- Fake/test data separation



Rules:

\- Customer-facing product/category pages should be indexable.

\- Admin/internal pages should not be indexable.

\- Product schema must use accurate public information only.

\- Do not add internal cost/supplier/scoring fields to structured data.

\- Separate MVP-now from later improvements.



Output:

1\. Missing before launch

2\. Nice-to-have after launch

3\. Exact files to edit

4\. Manual test checklist
